    Illustrative score: 38 out of 100. The headline number rolls up all 12 dimensions into one figure. In this sample, a 38 lands in the high-risk band. The report explains the two dimensions dragging it down the most, which here are concentration and correlation, so you know where to look first.

    2. Concentration finding

    Illustrative flag: high concentration. One coin, SOL at 40 percent, drives most of the downside. The report shows a concentration score and points out that a single high-beta position this large means the portfolio largely rises and falls with one coin. The sample flags SOL as the dominant risk driver.

    3. Correlation finding

    Illustrative flag: false diversification. SOL and the four layer-1 altcoins tend to move together, especially in a selloff. On paper this looks like six positions. After correlation, the report estimates roughly two to three independent bets. That is the gap between how diversified a portfolio looks and how it actually behaves.
